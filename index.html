      🎨 Make Focusly feel right

      Everything here is a preference, changeable any time from ⚙ → Sensory profile.

      Reading style

      These change how all text looks. Lexend and OpenDyslexic are fonts some readers find easier; roomy spacing and larger text reduce visual crowding.

      Motion

      Playful keeps spins, hops, and confetti. Minimal turns all of that off and keeps only calm highlights, if animations distract or overwhelm you.

      Focus sound · helps some people concentrate; plays only while this tab is open

      Steady background sound masks distracting noises. Evidence is mixed: it helps many ADHD brains focus and bothers others, so try one for ten minutes and trust your own reaction. All sounds are generated on your device.

      Read aloud

      Speaks Blox’s replies and quest steps out loud, in your browser’s voice. Helpful when reading is tiring or you absorb spoken words better. The 🔊 button on each step card always works, on or off.

      Companion (body doubling)

      Body doubling means working with someone quietly present - many people with ADHD focus better simply because someone is there. With this on, Blox stays visibly with you: a green dot on his avatar, a hello when you start, an occasional “still with you” line while you work, and one soft nudge if you go quiet. He never watches, judges, or reports anything.

      Blox's tone

      How Blox talks to you. Warm adds encouragement; brief strips it to just the facts, if cheer reads as noise.
